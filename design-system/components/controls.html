Buttons

Primary is ink on paper. Secondary is a hairline outline. Ghost is muted text that underlines on hover — used for destructive-adjacent or low-frequency actions like Undo last and Remove. The disabled primary is how a fully-spent pack presents: you cannot cut a notch you don't have.

Fields

Focus is an inset 2px underline in ink, like a pen resting on a ruled line — not a coloured glow. Optional and explanatory text sits inside the label in regular weight so the form reads as a sentence rather than a wall of required fields.
