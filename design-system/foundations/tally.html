The tally

The brand device and the primary information display, not a decoration. Sessions are drawn as real tally marks — four uprights crossed by a fifth diagonal, the way anyone counts on paper. Sessions still owed are struck in the health colour; sessions already delivered are ghosted back to the rule colour, so a pack reads as what's left rather than what's gone.

Why two runs, not one

Used and remaining are drawn as two separate well-formed tallies rather than one continuous run that changes colour partway. This is the single most important rule in the component.

Rejected — one continuous run

Pack sizes are nearly always multiples of five, so the last remaining mark lands on the group's diagonal. "One session left" — the state the whole product exists to surface — renders as a lone slash, the least legible mark in the set.

Shipped — two runs

The remainder always starts at the first upright of its own group, so it reads cleanly at any count. Nine spent, one left.

Limits

Above 40 sessions the marks stop being scannable and the component renders nothing — the numeral carries it, with a plain rule beneath so the layout doesn't collapse. Never draw a tally for a figure that sums several packs: a proportional mark would imply a pack size that doesn't exist.
