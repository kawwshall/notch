Layout rules

  • Rules, never shadows. Hairlines at --rule separate everything.
  • Corners are 3px. Nothing in this product is a pill.
  • Asymmetric grids on marketing pages; no three-across card rows.
  • No icons. If something needs an icon to be understood, rewrite the label.
  • Section headers are .label over a rule, not a type step.
